# No numeric quality scores in the gates

The loop gates check presence and order: a validation after the last change, a verdict with its required sections, a terminal-state line from the declared set. Gates that emit a quality score, a confidence percentage or a letter grade are out of scope.

## Why this is out of scope

A score invites the reading the banner exists to prevent: that the gate measured quality. It measured shape. A run can pass every gate and still build the wrong integration; only the reviewer's findings and the person's judgement say whether the work is good. A number on the gate would be invented precision, and invented precision gets trusted.

Every gate message opens with "GATE RESULT ONLY, presence and order, not evidence of quality." for exactly this reason.

## What would have to change

A measurement the gate can actually make, with a stated method, such as the validation error count it already quotes. Anything beyond that stays with the reviewer.
